# Research Notes – Task Estimation in Scrum

## Source 1
- **Title:** Why Software Estimation is Hard
- **Author / Site:** Scrum.org
- **Link:** https://www.scrum.org/resources/blog/why-estimation-hard
- **Key points:**
  - Software tasks are unpredictable due to unknown complexity
  - Developers often underestimate effort
  - Hidden work is a major issue
- **Useful theme:** Estimation uncertainty

## Source 2
- **Title:** Agile Estimation Best Practices
- **Author / Site:** Atlassian
- **Link:** https://www.atlassian.com/agile/project-management/estimation
- **Key points:**
  - Estimation should be team-based
  - Use relative sizing instead of time
  - Break tasks into smaller pieces
- **Useful theme:** Team estimation and relative sizing

## Source 3
- **Title:** Story Points vs Hours
- **Author / Site:** Mountain Goat Software
- **Link:** https://www.mountaingoatsoftware.com/blog/story-points
- **Key points:**
  - Story points reflect complexity, not time
  - Time estimates create false precision
  - Relative estimation is more consistent
- **Useful theme:** Relative vs time-based estimation

## Source 4
- **Title:** Why Your Estimates Are Always Wrong
- **Author / Site:** Medium (Developer Blog)
- **Link:** https://medium.com
- **Key points:**
  - Engineers forget testing and debugging time
  - Overconfidence leads to poor estimates
  - Pressure causes unrealistic estimates
- **Useful theme:** Human bias in estimation

## Source 5
- **Title:** Agile Estimation Anti-Patterns
- **Author / Site:** ThoughtWorks
- **Link:** https://www.thoughtworks.com
- **Key points:**
  - Estimation should not be used as performance measurement
  - Large tasks lead to inaccurate estimates
  - Lack of discussion leads to poor estimates
- **Useful theme:** Bad estimation practices

---

## Common Themes
- Estimation is always uncertain
- Team discussion improves accuracy
- Relative estimation is better than time-based
- Breaking work into smaller tasks improves estimation

## Bad Practices Mentioned Repeatedly
- Estimating alone
- Ignoring hidden work
- Treating estimates as deadlines
- Overconfidence

## Good Practices Mentioned Repeatedly
- Team-based estimation
- Use story points or relative sizing
- Break tasks down
- Re-evaluate estimates regularly
